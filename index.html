Real-Time Listening Behavior Generation for Social Robots via Decoupled Timing and LLM Reasoning

The robot's timing and its wording come from separate models: a transformer predicts when to respond, a fine-tuned Gemma4-2B decides what to say. These pages show seven cases against three baselines, the same input under three listener styles, and five runs live on a Pepper.

1. Context-appropriate responses

↑ Top
  • Proposed = Transformer-based timing model with our fine-tuned Gemma4-2B.
  • AnnoReplay = the annotated listener behaviour replayed on the robot.
  • Random = the same behaviours as AnnoReplay, emitted at random times.
  • VAP-Nod+BC = three nod types and a single “yeah” as its only verbal response, with no idle behaviour.

The trace shows the selected verbal backchannels, reactive nods, idle head and idle body; the blue text is the user transcript the LLM was given when that verbal backchannel was chosen. Ratings are the mean of three comparison questions (attentiveness, appropriateness, human-likeness) on −2…+2, averaged over n pairwise trials; each number is how much that method was preferred over its counterpart. “not compared” = that pair was not covered in the user study. Backchannels and reactive nods are not executed while another behaviour is still running on the same channel, which the head shares with idle movements; idle head and body have no threshold and fire stochastically with the predicted probability. AnnoReplay loses 44.3% of the annotated gestures, two thirds to execution overlap and one third to embodiment limits.

4. Live on Pepper

↑ Top
  • Input: the same five inputs as §1, streamed without a transcript; each card keeps its case number there.
  • Robot: joint targets streamed over TCP to a NAOqi bridge on Pepper.
  • Hardware: a single laptop GPU (RTX 4070 Laptop, 8 GB).
  • Pipeline: ASR (faster-whisper + sherpa-onnx), timing model, Gemma4-2B, Chatterbox TTS.
  • Latency: ASR 549 ms, LLM 367 ms, timing model 6.3 ms per frame.
  • Context lag: 1,105 ms, 4.0 words behind the speaker.

Latencies are means over the five cases; the buffer wait is how long a word sits before the next decision, so ASR + LLM + wait = the context lag. Red words in the transcript are ASR errors, or words that arrived too late for that decision. The grey line in parentheses is what the same model chose from the ground-truth transcript instead of ASR. TTS audio is pre-synthesised for the expressions the model uses most.
